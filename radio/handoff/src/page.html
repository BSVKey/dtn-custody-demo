Offline payment handoff

BSVKey

How to use this page

Send a Bitcoin BSV payment to someone nearby with no internet: as a QR code, as sound, or as a text message. Everything happens on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you press "Broadcast now" ("Get my coins" only reads your balance).

Try it first, on one phone (1 minute)
  1. On Pay, press Try a demo payment. It makes a practice payment that is signed but not spendable.
  2. Press Copy text.
  3. Open , paste it into the box at the bottom and press Check it.
  4. You will see "Signed correctly": that is the check the other person's phone does with no internet.
Pay someone face to face (QR code)
  1. You: on Pay, open Send real BSV: load your wallet, press Get my coins while you still have internet, enter the address and amount, and press Sign payment. (Or paste a payment you already signed.)
  2. You: press Show QR code and hold the screen up.
  3. Them: on , enter their address and the amount, then press Scan QR code.
  4. Them: the page shows "pays you N sat" if it checks out. The payment waits under "Waiting to be sent".
  5. Them, later: when they have signal, press Text to gateway or Broadcast now. Only then is it final.
Pay by sound (phone to phone, walkie-talkie, radio, phone call)
  1. Them: on , press Listen and allow the microphone.
  2. You: on Pay, press Play as sound, with the phones close together. It plays twice, about 26 seconds each time.
  3. Over a walkie-talkie, an AM radio or a phone call, first pick the walkie-talkie option in . Hold the talk button while it plays (about 40 seconds).
  4. Them: the page shows "Heard part 1 of 3...", then checks the payment, just like a scan.
Pay by text message, and get a receipt
  1. In , enter the gateway's phone number and its public key (the gateway operator gives you both).
  2. On Pay, load the payment and press Text to gateway. Your messaging app opens with the payment filled in. Send it. This works with no mobile data, only texting.
  3. The gateway texts back a receipt. Paste it into Check the gateway's receipt. "ACCEPTED" means the network took the payment.
Get coins from a faucet
  1. On , enter your address, then Listen or Scan when a faucet is being announced (or paste its message).
  2. It appears under Faucets heard.
  3. For a normal faucet, press Claim by text and send the message. The faucet texts back what it paid you.
  4. For a "bearer" coin, press Sweep to my address, then forward it from "Waiting to be sent" as soon as you can. The first person to get a coin to the network keeps it.
Use it with no internet at all
  1. Open this page while you still have a connection, and keep the tab open, or download it to your phone and open the saved file.
  2. Everything above works offline. Only "Broadcast now" needs a connection, and texting needs a phone signal.

Good to know: a payment checked offline is signed correctly and pays the right amount, but it cannot be known final until it reaches the network. Treat it like a cheque: fine for small amounts, wait for a receipt for large ones.

      What this page can and cannot check

      With no internet it checks every signature and that the payment pays the amount it says. It cannot know whether the payer already spent the same coins somewhere else. Only a broadcast settles that, so for larger amounts wait for the gateway's ACCEPTED receipt.

      The page makes no network requests except when you press "Broadcast now" (to GorillaPool ARC) or "Get my coins" (it reads your address's coins from WhatsOnChain).
